Q. A particle moves in a straight line with uniform acceleration. If it covers distances s₁ and s₂ in consecutive equal time intervals t, then its uniform acceleration a is given by:

A (s₂ - s₁) / t²
B (s₂ - s₁) / (2t²)
C 2(s₂ - s₁) / t²
D (s₁ + s₂) / (2t²)
Verified Solution Key Concept: Kinematics Equations

Let initial velocity be u.
Distance in 1st interval: s₁ = ut + ½at²
Total distance in 2t: s₁ + s₂ = u(2t) + ½a(2t)² = 2ut + 2at²
Multiply 1st equation by 2: 2s₁ = 2ut + at²
Subtracting gives: s₂ - s₁ = at² ⇒ a = (s₂ - s₁) / t².
Correct Option: A

Solid Cylinder Rolling on Accelerating Rough Plank

A uniform solid cylinder of mass M and radius R rests on a horizontal plank of mass m. A horizontal force F is applied to the plank. The plank moves on a frictionless floor, but friction between the cylinder and plank is sufficient to prevent slipping. Which of the following statements is/are CORRECT?

✓ A: Friction force on the cylinder acts in the forward direction (same as force F).
✓ B: The acceleration of the cylinder's center of mass is a_c = F / (3m + M).
✓ C: The angular acceleration of the cylinder is α = 2 F / [R(3m + M)].
✗ D: The friction force between cylinder and plank does non-zero negative total work on the combined system.
Marking: +4 (A, B, C) • -2 If D Selected
Correct Options: (A, B, C)
Derivation:
1. Let f be the friction force on cylinder. For forward angular acceleration α, f must provide forward linear acceleration and clockwise torque about COM: f = M a_c and f R = (1/2 M R²) α ⇒ R α = 2 a_c.
2. Non-slipping constraint at contact point: a_plank = a_c + R α = a_c + 2 a_c = 3 a_c.
3. Plank equation: F - f = m a_plank ⇒ F - M a_c = m(3 a_c) ⇒ a_c = F / (3m + M).
4. Since rolling is without slipping, relative velocity at contact is zero, so static friction does zero net work on the system. Hence D is FALSE.

Conducting Rod on Conducting Rails with Magnetic Field

A conducting rod of length L = 1 m and mass m = 0.5 kg slides without friction on two parallel horizontal rails separated by L in a uniform vertical magnetic field B = 2 T. A resistor R = 4 Ω connects the rails. If the rod is given an initial velocity v₀ = 8 m/s, the total distance traveled by the rod before coming to rest is:

Verified Result: Option C (8 m)
Core Equation: Retarding force F = - B I L = - (B² L² / R) v.
Differential Form: m (dv/dt) = - (B² L² / R) v ⇒ m (v dv/dx) = - (B² L² / R) v ⇒ m dv = - (B² L² / R) dx.
Integrating from v = v₀ to 0: m (0 - v₀) = - (B² L² / R) x ⇒ x = (m v₀ R) / (B² L²).
Substitution: x = (0.5 × 8 × 4) / (2² × 1²) = 16 / 4 = 4 meters [Wait: 0.5 * 8 * 4 = 16; 16/4 = 4 m! Option B].

Stereoisomerism & Spin in Octahedral [Co(en)₂Cl₂]⁺

For the complex cation [Co(en)₂Cl₂]⁺ (where en = ethane-1,2-diamine and Co has atomic number 27), which of the following statements is/are CORRECT?

✓ A: The cis-isomer is optically active and exists as a pair of non-superimposable enantiomers.
✓ B: The trans-isomer has a center of inversion and a plane of symmetry, making it optically inactive (achiral).
✓ C: The central Co³⁺ ion has d²sp³ hybridization and is diamagnetic (low spin d⁶).
✗ D: The total number of stereoisomers (geometrical + optical) is equal to 4.
Marking: +4 (A, B, C) • -2 If D Selected
Correct Options: (A, B, C)
Analysis:
1. [Co(en)₂Cl₂]⁺ has cis and trans geometrical isomers.
2. Cis-isomer lacks any plane or center of inversion; it exists as dextro (d) and laevo (l) enantiomeric forms (Optically Active).

4. Total stereoisomers = 1 trans + 2 cis enantiomers = 3 stereoisomers (NOT 4, hence D is FALSE).

Phenol Synthesis via Cumene & Electrophilic Tribromination

Cumene (isopropylbenzene) is treated with O₂ followed by hydrolysis with dilute H₂SO₄ to produce Compound X and Compound Y. Compound X gives a white precipitate with bromine water to yield Compound Z. Which statements is/are CORRECT?

✓ A: Compound X is Phenol (C₆H₅OH) and Compound Y is Acetone (CH₃COCH₃).

✗ D: Compound X reacts with neutral FeCl₃ to give a yellow coloration.

Mathematics • Definite Integrals IIT Advanced 2024 Paper 2

Leibniz Rule of Differentiation under the Integral Sign

Let f(x) = ∫₀^(x²) [ (sin t) / √t ] dt for x > 0. Then the value of the derivative f'(x) at x = √π is:

Verified Result: Option B (Value = 0)
Leibniz Differentiation Rule:
d/dx [ ∫_{u(x)}^{v(x)} g(t) dt ] = g(v(x)) · v'(x) - g(u(x)) · u'(x).
Here u(x) = 0 and v(x) = x² (v'(x) = 2x).
f'(x) = [ sin(x²) / √(x²) ] · (2x) = [ sin(x²) / x ] · 2x = 2 sin(x²).
At x = √π: f'(√π) = 2 sin((√π)²) = 2 sin(π) = 2(0) = 0.
